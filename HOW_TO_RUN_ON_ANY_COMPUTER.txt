================================================================
  SPIDY CRYPTO - SETUP ON ANY COMPUTER / LAPTOP
================================================================

Step 1: Install Python
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Download Python from https://www.python.org/downloads/
   (Version 3.11 or 3.12 recommended)
2. IMPORTANT during installation:
   [X] Check the box that says "Add python.exe to PATH" !

Step 2: Extract SPIDY CRYPTO
----------------------------------------------------------------
Extract "SPIDY_CRYPTO_PORTABLE.zip" into any folder on your computer
(for example: C:\spidy_crypto or on your Desktop).

Step 3: Install Required Libraries (One-time setup)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click:
  INSTALL_DEPENDENCIES.bat

Step 4: Launch SPIDY CRYPTO
----------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click:
  RUN_SPIDY.bat

This will:
- Launch the FastAPI Trading Engine
- Connect to Delta Exchange India live WebSocket
- Connect to your Telegram Bot (@Spidy67bot)
- Open your browser dashboard automatically at http://127.0.0.1:8800

Enjoy automated trading intelligence!
